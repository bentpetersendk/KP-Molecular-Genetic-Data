## Before the first practical

1. Create a free account on [Galaxy Europe](https://usegalaxy.eu/login/start) and log in.
2. Read [Getting started](galaxy/index.md) if you have not used Galaxy before.
3. Open the practical from the [Practicals](exercises/index.md) page and follow it step by step.
   Write down your answers to the questions as you go.
